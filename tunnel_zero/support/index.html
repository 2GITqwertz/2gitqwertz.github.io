Tunnel:Zero Support

Fast, focused gameplay on your wrist.

Tunnel:Zero is a standalone watchOS game for Apple Watch. This page provides technical help, answers to common questions, and a way to share feedback.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to play Tunnel:Zero?

Tunnel:Zero requires an Apple Watch running watchOS 10 or later.

How do I turn off the soundtrack?

Open Options in Tunnel:Zero to enable or disable the soundtrack. Disabling the soundtrack does not disable gameplay sounds or haptics.

Where are my personal records stored?

Your personal records and settings are stored locally on your Apple Watch. They are not transferred from the device.

What happens if I delete and reinstall the app?

Deleting Tunnel:Zero removes all locally stored records and settings. Reinstalling the app starts with no saved records or preferences.

What can I do if sound or haptics behave unexpectedly?

Check the sound and haptic settings on your Apple Watch, then close and restart Tunnel:Zero.

Privacy

Tunnel:Zero does not collect or transmit personal data. Read the Tunnel:Zero Privacy Policy for details.
