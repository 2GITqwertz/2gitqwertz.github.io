Privacy Policy

Tunnel:Zero for Apple Watch

This policy explains what Tunnel:Zero stores on your Apple Watch and how that information is handled.

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Data Collection

Tunnel:Zero is a standalone watchOS game. It does not require a user account and does not collect or transmit personal data. Gameplay data is not transferred from your Apple Watch.

Data Stored on Your Apple Watch

Tunnel:Zero stores only the following information locally on your Apple Watch:

  • Best Score
  • Best Stage
  • Maximum Streak
  • Most Bonuses
  • The record display most recently selected on the start screen
  • The soundtrack setting

How the Data Is Used

The locally stored information is used only to preserve your records and settings between gameplay sessions. Tunnel:Zero does not provide cloud-based synchronization.

Data Sharing and Tracking

Tunnel:Zero does not share data with third parties. It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app has no in-app purchases or paid subscriptions.

Data Retention and Deletion

Records and settings remain stored locally until you delete Tunnel:Zero. Deleting the app removes all data stored by Tunnel:Zero from your Apple Watch.

Children’s Privacy

Tunnel:Zero does not collect or transmit personal data from any user, including children. All records and settings listed above remain on the Apple Watch until the app is deleted.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Tunnel:Zero or its data practices change. Any update will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, email tunnelzero@2git.de. For technical help, visit Tunnel:Zero Support.
